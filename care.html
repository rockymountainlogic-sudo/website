How Rocky Mountain Logic & Care works

Every sit is approached like a small system: animals, property, and routines all need to stay steady while you’re gone. I keep things calm, predictable, and well-documented.

Pet care & routines

Consistent feeding, walks, meds (per your vet), enrichment, and quiet time. I’m experienced with seniors, anxiety, and reactivity, and I follow your written routines closely so your animals feel safe and heard.

Home & property care

Lights, blinds, mail, trash, snow, plants, and garden. I keep things tidy, handle small issues, and flag anything that needs attention before it becomes a problem.

Mountain & rural sits

Comfortable with snow, wildlife, and off-pavement living. I’ve cared for cabins, goats, ducks, chickens, and more, with weather and security check-ins baked into the routine.

Tech-aware home monitoring

As an IT & AV consultant, I can keep an eye on your cameras, network, and smart devices, and help you troubleshoot if something acts up while you’re away.

Clear communication

We’ll agree on how often you want updates. I send photos, short notes, and simple status checklists so you always know how everyone and everything is doing.

Flexible length & scope

From weekend trips to multi-week travel, I adapt to your schedule and needs, including staggered arrivals, overlapping guests, or special projects during your trip.
