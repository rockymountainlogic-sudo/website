What this assessment does

This tool helps you see the current state of your agency in a structured way. It looks at compliance, documentation, Therap setup, DVR or HCBS workflows, administrative systems, and basic technology hygiene.

The goal is not to grade you. The goal is to give you a clear picture and a practical path forward.

  • Readiness score and risk level
  • Summary of strengths and areas to strengthen
  • Recommended support tier that matches your pace and capacity
  • Downloadable summary you can keep for your records
  • Option to receive a customized proposal from Rocky Mountain Logic

Who it is for

This assessment is designed for:

  • PASA agencies and agencies seeking PASA approval
  • DVR vendors and providers building out service lines
  • HCBS day programs and community based programs
  • Organizations using Therap or planning to move to Therap
  • Small human service organizations that are growing and need more structure

Whether you are just getting started or have been operating for years, this can help you see the full picture of your systems and where small changes will have the most impact.

How it works

  1. Complete the assessment online. It takes about 5 to 10 minutes.
  2. Receive a readiness score, risk level, and a recommended support tier.
  3. Download a summary you can review with your team or board.
  4. Optionally, schedule time with Rocky Mountain Logic to walk through your results and plan next steps.

The questions are written in plain language. The experience is meant to reduce anxiety, not raise it.
